Privacy Policy — Xerji

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What data the app handles

Xerji stores the information you enter yourself: expenses, categories, budgets, salary plan, debts, installments, savings groups (jamiya), rent and savings, plus optional phone numbers you add for contacts.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally on your phone and/or computer. The developer does not run any server and never receives your data.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to enable sync, the app asks for the drive.appdata permission only. This lets the app create and read a single file in a hidden app-specific folder of your own Google Drive. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files. The file is used only to keep your own devices in sync. You can disconnect at any time from the app settings, and you can delete the hidden data from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Use of Google user data

Xerji's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
